ATM Withdrawal Flowchart

This chart shows every step when a customer takes cash from an ATM. It goes from putting in the card to taking the card back.

  • 69 steps
  • 10 Terminators
  • 25 Input / Output
  • 20 Process
  • 14 Decisions

The five symbols

Each flowchart symbol has a specific meaning and purpose.

  • Terminator

    Shows where the chart starts or ends.

    ExampleStart, End

  • Input / Output

    Shows when information goes into or out of the system.

    ExampleCustomer enters the PIN

  • Process

    Shows an action or a calculation.

    ExampleAdd 1 to PIN tries

  • Decision

    Asks a question with a Yes path and a No path.

    ExampleIs the PIN correct?

  • Arrow

    Shows the direction and the order of the flow.

    ExampleFollow the arrows
